Welcome parents, teachers, and young learners! Help your child master the 26 letters of the English Alphabet with this free, colorful interactive game. Kids can click the sound button to hear the English pronunciation, match letters to pictures (from Apple to Zebra), and practice Uppercase (Big) and Lowercase (Small) letters!

📌 Lesson Summary: The English Alphabet

  • 26 Letters: There are 26 letters in the English alphabet (from A to Z).
  • Uppercase Letters (Big): A, B, C, D, E, F, G... used at the start of sentences and names.
  • Lowercase Letters (Small): a, b, c, d, e, f, g... used for regular words.
